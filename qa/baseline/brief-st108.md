- **Paragraph 1:** ANSI/AAMI ST108 is the first comprehensive U.S. standard focused specifically on the quality of water used for processing medical devices. - **Paragraph 2:** The standard recognizes water quality itself as an important patient safety variable in medical device processing.
- **List heading:** ST108 water quality management includes:
