- **H2:** What Is ST108?
- **Paragraph 1:** ANSI/AAMI ST108 is the first comprehensive U.S. standard focused specifically on the quality of water used for processing medical devices. It establishes requirements for water quality management programs, including testing, monitoring, documentation, review, and corrective actions.
- **Paragraph 2:** The standard recognizes water quality itself as an important patient safety variable in medical device processing.
- **List heading:** ST108 water quality management includes:
- **List item 1:** Assessing current water systems and quality
- **List item 2:** Implementing a water management plan
- **List item 3:** Validating that systems meet water quality requirements
- **List item 4:** Routinely monitoring water quality
